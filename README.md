Extract verified remote tech jobs, company logos, categories, regions, employment types, and direct job links from WeWorkRemotely.

Powered by the official WeWorkRemotely RSS feed: Fast, structured, zero browser overhead, zero proxy fees.

---

### Features
- **Direct Job & Application URLs:** Instant extraction of hiring links.
- **Regions & Employment Types:** Filter by Worldwide or regional restrictions.
- **Pay-Per-Event:** Only $0.003 per extracted lead. Zero compute surcharges.

---

### Integrations
- **Google Sheets & Airtable:** Export tech talent leads for headhunters and founders.
- **Make & Zapier:** Instant alerts on new remote openings.
- **n8n:** Custom lead enrichment pipelines.
